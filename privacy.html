Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Sejarah Shorts is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own TikTok account.

What data the app uses

  • TikTok authorisation tokens for the owner's account, with the user.info.basic and video.upload scopes. They are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok drafts.
  • The app does not collect, store or share personal data of any other person, does not read followers, comments, messages or analytics, and does not use cookies or tracking.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) and by deleting the stored secrets.
